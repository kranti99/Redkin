Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 1, 2026

Redkin ("we," "our," or "us") operates the Redkin mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, disclosure, and deletion of personal data when you use our App.


1. Information We Collect

To operate our blood donation finder service, we collect the following personal and sensitive user data:

  • Personal Details: Full name, phone number, and email address for account creation and user verification.
  • Health & Sensitive Data: Blood type and donation eligibility status to connect donors with receivers.
  • Location Data: Precise or approximate location (GPS/Network) to display nearby blood requests and locate nearby eligible donors. Location data is accessed only when you use location-based features.
  • User Profile Data: Profile details, emergency blood request posts, and availability status.

2. How We Use Your Data

We collect and use your data strictly for the following purposes:

  • To register and authenticate your user account.
  • To search and display nearby donors or emergency blood requests based on blood group and proximity.
  • To send push notifications regarding urgent blood requests in your area.
  • To allow peer-to-peer communication between donors and requestors.
  • To maintain application security, troubleshoot issues, and prevent fraud.

3. Data Sharing and Visibility

Public Visibility within App: Your contact phone number, blood type, general distance/location, and donor status are displayed to registered users who create or view emergency blood requests to facilitate rapid contact.

No Commercial Sharing: We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information, contact numbers, or health data to advertisers or third-party marketing brokers.


4. Third-Party Services

The App integrates third-party services that may collect information used to identify your device or process data securely:

  • Google Play Services / Firebase Authentication: Account creation and secure session management.
  • Firebase Realtime Database / Cloud Storage: Storing donor profiles and broadcast notifications.
  • Google Maps API: Calculating distances and rendering map locations for nearby donors.

5. User Data Security

We prioritize your privacy and data security. All data transmitted between the App and our backend servers is encrypted in transit using standard Secure Sockets Layer (SSL/TLS) technology.


6. Account & Data Deletion Policy

You have full control over your personal data. You can request the complete deletion of your account and associated personal data at any time through two methods:

  • In-App Deletion: Go to Settings > Account > Delete Account within the Redkin App.
  • Web Deletion Request: If you have uninstalled the app or cannot access it, visit our dedicated Data Deletion Request Page to request complete deletion of your profile, contact details, blood group data, and location records.

Upon receiving a valid deletion request, your account record, active blood requests, and personal identifiers will be permanently removed from our active database within 30 days.


7. Children's Privacy

Redkin does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from anyone under the age of 18. If we learn that we have collected personal data from a minor, we will delete that information immediately.
